Help & Guide

Learn how to use the Virtual e-Office simulator.

About this Simulator

Virtual e-Office is a fictional training environment designed to help users practice electronic office procedures before working with a real e-Office system.

Important

This application does not connect to any government e-Office system and does not contain real government data.
